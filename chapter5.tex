\subsection{Major Chemistry Breakthrough: Carbon-Free Sandwich Molecule}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{The Breakthrough:} Researchers at \textbf{IIT Madras} and the \textbf{Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru}, have achieved a major chemistry breakthrough by synthesizing a completely \textbf{carbon-free} molecule that replicates the unique sandwich structure of the famous carbon-containing \textbf{ferrocene} molecule.
\end{itemize}
